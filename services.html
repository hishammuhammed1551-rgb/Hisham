Our services

Shipping, clearing and customs.

Everything you need to move goods in and out of the UAE, handled by one experienced team in Khor Fakkan.

01

Shipping

Getting your cargo from origin to destination.

We arrange the transport of your goods and look after the bookings and documentation, so you don't have to deal with multiple carriers yourself.

  • Sea freight: full container (FCL) and shared container (LCL)
  • Air freight for urgent shipments
  • Land transport within the UAE and the GCC
  • Import and export shipments
02

Clearing

Releasing your goods from the port without delays.

Once your cargo arrives, we handle the port and terminal procedures needed to get it released and on its way.

  • Port and terminal clearance
  • Delivery orders and container release
  • Coordination with shipping lines and port authorities
  • Arranging delivery to your warehouse or site
03

Customs

Accurate customs paperwork, done for you.

We prepare and submit your customs declarations and make sure your documents are complete and correct, helping you avoid hold-ups and penalties.

  • Import and export customs declarations
  • Document checks: invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin
  • Duty and fee processing
  • Advice on customs requirements for your goods
FAQ

Common questions

What documents do I need for customs clearance?

Usually a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill, and a certificate of origin. Some goods need extra permits. Contact us with your shipment details and we'll tell you exactly what's required.

Do you handle both imports and exports?

Yes. We support shipments coming into the UAE and shipments leaving it.

How long does clearance take?

It depends on the type of goods, the port and whether all documents are ready. Sending us complete documents early is the best way to keep things fast.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the form on our contact page, or call or email us with your shipment details. We'll reply with a quote.

Need help with a shipment?

Tell us what you're moving and we'll recommend the right service.
