About us

Logistics made simple, from Khor Fakkan.

Lowcaps is a logistics company located in Khor Fakkan, United Arab Emirates, providing shipping, clearing and customs services to businesses and individuals.

Who we are

A local team you can rely on

Moving goods across borders involves a lot of steps: bookings, documents, inspections, duties and port procedures. A delay in any one of them can hold up your whole shipment.

At Lowcaps, we bring those steps together under one roof. Our team handles the shipping, the clearing and the customs work, and keeps you informed along the way.

Our goal is simple: get your cargo where it needs to be, on time, with no surprises.

“Your shipment is our responsibility, from the moment you contact us until it reaches you.”
The Lowcaps team
