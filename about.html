About us

Advisors who care about what happens next.

Since 2010, Meridian Advisory has helped hundreds of businesses grow with clarity, discipline, and confidence.

Our story

Built on a simple idea

Meridian was founded by a group of former Big Four consultants who saw too many growing companies receive expensive advice they couldn't use.

We set out to build a different kind of firm: senior people on every project, honest conversations, and recommendations designed to be implemented by real teams with real constraints.

Today we work with founders, family businesses, and mid-market companies across a dozen industries, and most of our new clients come from referrals.

“We measure our success by our clients' results, not the number of slides we produce.”
Founding principle, 2010
